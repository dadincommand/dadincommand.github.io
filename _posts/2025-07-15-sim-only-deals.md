For years, I was the mug who always had the latest phone, paying top dollar every month like I was made of money. That was fine when it was just me. But now I’ve got a whole rugby team of kids and three of them already need phones — one of them to monitor her diabetes — and another is writing “MOBILE PHONE” in bold on his Christmas list to Santa. Suddenly, that £50+ a month per contract adds up real quick.

So I had a dad epiphany: **new phones don’t do anything groundbreaking**. They just do the same stuff — scrolling, texting, calls, and the occasional dinosaur game — but a bit faster. As long as you’re not stuck with a Nokia 3210 (iconic, but let’s not lie to ourselves), an older Android will do the job.

Take mine for example: I'm running this entire blog from an old Samsung A71 that I flashed a custom ROM on. It's now rocking Android 15 and it works like a dream.

### The Plan: Keep Your Phone, Dump the Contract

When my last contract ended, instead of getting locked into another 24 or 36-month money pit, I jumped on a SIM-only deal. At first, I found Smarty Mobile: £7 a month for 8GB of data. Not bad. Then I added more family members, and Smarty gives you 10%–20% off per extra SIM. We were all on it eventually.

But — because life — those costs crept up. Again.

So I went hunting. I trawled the comparison sites, read forums, checked cashback offers. I was willing to go full bargain-hunter mode, and I found **a unicorn** on **MoneySavingExpert**:

- **Provider:** Lebara  
- **Deal:** 15GB data, Unlimited minutes/texts, *+* 150 international minutes  
- **Price:** **£0.35 a month.**  
- No typo. Thirty-five pence.

It’s a promo price for 7 months, after which it jumps to £7.95. But the beauty of 1-month rolling contracts? You just **switch again** before that happens.

### My Household Phone Bill: £2.10 for 6 Phones

Right now, **six** of us are on rolling SIM-only deals. We’ve got unlimited calls and texts, and more data than any of them use (they're on Wi-Fi all day anyway). And it’s costing me a grand total of **£2.10 a month**.

Let me say that again for the dads in the back: **£2.10 for 6 active SIMs.**

### Final Thoughts (a.k.a Dad Wisdom)

- **Keep your phone.** If it works, don't bin it.
- **Custom ROMs** can breathe life into older phones (just know what you're doing).
- **Look for short-term promo deals** — especially on sites like MoneySavingExpert.
- **Avoid long contracts** like the plague.
- **Switch often**. There's no loyalty reward in telecoms, only punishment for staying.

In a world where everything's going up, your phone bill doesn’t have to.  
Be the smart dad. Or mum. Or cool uncle. You get the idea.
