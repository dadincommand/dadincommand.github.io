  <h2>About This Site</h2>
<p>Hey, I'm Jonny — former soldier, full-time dad, and the slightly frazzled fella behind this site. With eight brilliant (and occasionally feral) kids, life’s never quiet, clean, or on schedule — but it’s always full of heart.</p>

<p>This site is my way of sharing what I've learned the hard way — from breastfeeding support with the <strong><a href="app.md">Mamma's Milk Safe to Feed Timer</a></strong>, to keeping a big family fed, entertained, and mostly alive on a budget.</p>

<h3>What You'll Find Here:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>👶 The App:</strong> A timer to help breastfeeding mums enjoy a safe sip now and then — guilt-free, easy to use, and made with real-life in mind.</li>
  <li><strong>📖 The Blog:</strong> True stories from the Dad Front — think disasters, victories, and a few tips along the way.</li>
  <li><strong>🎒 Family Life:</strong> Money-saving tips, holiday ideas, and what happens when your toddler calls you "bruh."</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why I Built This</h3>
<p>Honestly? Because there wasn’t anything quite like it. Mums needed something practical. Dads needed something honest. Parents needed something real. So here it is: a no-fluff, real-talk hub for navigating the madness of modern family life — especially when your squad outnumbers a football team.</p>

<p>If you're a parent winging it with love, welcome aboard.</p>
